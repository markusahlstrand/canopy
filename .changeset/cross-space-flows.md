Flows are no longer part of a space. A flow belongs to the install, can watch several spaces and copy into others, and is tagged with every space it touches. You see and edit a flow when you own all of its spaces. Flows open from the sidebar, and from a space's settings filtered to that space.

Flows are now drawn on a canvas. A check has a Yes and a No path, so one flow can send statements straight to the gestoría and hold everything else for your approval. "Ask me first" is now a "Wait for your approval" step you can put on one path only, and what comes before it runs straight away. The preview shows how many documents reach each step.

Flows saved in a space before this move out of it on their own the next time that space's automation runs, as a straight path: each condition's Yes leads on, and "Ask me first" becomes a wait before the actions. What they already did stays.
