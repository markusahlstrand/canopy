A file's or folder's menu gathers the AI actions under one "Reprocess" submenu, so running the classification again is easy to find: "Label with AI" (and on a folder "Re-label all with AI") re-runs Document AI's label, description and category, and "Find people and companies" reads the document again. "Find people and companies" now works on a single file too, and connected spaces (a3doc, Qonto, buckets) offer the Reprocess items as well, since a synced file's label and category belong to the space.
