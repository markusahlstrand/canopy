Parent: [#78](https://github.com/markusahlstrand/canopy/issues/78). Also tracks #22; the built-in search decision is documented in `documentation/03-how-plugins-work.md`.

## Correction

A refused or failed results refresh now clears earlier hits and their selection, displays the failed-view recovery action, and retries the current query.
