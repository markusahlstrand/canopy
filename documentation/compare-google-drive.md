# Google Drive

> The default consumer cloud drive — deeply tied to Google Workspace and Gmail.

## What it is

A hosted drive with best-in-class real-time collaboration (Docs / Sheets / Slides), excellent web,
mobile, and desktop apps, powerful search, and Gemini AI baked in. Your files live in Google's
cloud under your Google account.

## Where it beats Canopy

- Polish and reliability at planetary scale; nothing to run.
- Real-time co-editing and commenting across the Office-equivalents.
- First-class iOS / Android apps and a desktop sync client (Drive for desktop).
- Mature sharing, link controls, and search; integrated AI.

## Where Canopy differs

- Your bytes live in storage **you** own (R2 / filesystem, or an indexed bucket), not Google's.
- Self-hostable and edge-deployable; no vendor account — auth is your own OIDC provider.
- Extensible with your own **sandboxed plugins**, not just Workspace Marketplace add-ons.
- Open source (MIT); you can read and change every line.

## Pick Google Drive if

You want the most polished, zero-ops experience with great mobile and collaboration, and you're
comfortable with your files on Google's servers.
