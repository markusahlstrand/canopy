# Paperless-ngx

> Self-hosted document management — scan, OCR, auto-tag, and full-text search your paperwork.

## What it is

A self-hosted DMS focused on documents: it ingests scans / PDFs, runs **OCR**, auto-classifies
with tags / correspondents / document types, and provides full-text search and an archive. It's
specialised for paperwork, not a general drive.

## Where it beats Canopy

- A purpose-built document pipeline: OCR, tagging rules, correspondents, full-text search.
- Mature workflows for digitising and organising paper.

## Where Canopy differs

- Canopy is a **general** drive / portal. Its **Document AI** auto-labels and describes uploads — a
  lighter, more general take than Paperless's OCR + tagging — and full-text search isn't built yet.
- Edge-native, plugin-extensible, with sharing and spaces; not a dedicated DMS.
- The directions rhyme: Canopy's content-types + AI labeling head toward some of what Paperless
  does well today.

## Pick Paperless-ngx if

Your core need is digitising and searching documents (receipts, invoices, letters) with OCR and
tagging.
