# Synology (DSM)

> A NAS operating system — a whole storage appliance, usually on Synology hardware.

## What it is

DiskStation Manager: a polished NAS OS with RAID / SHR storage, snapshots, backups (Hyper Backup),
and a package center — Synology Drive, Photos, and Office (with real-time co-editing) — plus Drive
desktop and mobile clients. Tied to Synology boxes.

## Where it beats Canopy

- It manages the **disks**: RAID, snapshots, scrubbing, backup orchestration.
- A full appliance: media, photos, surveillance, VMs / containers, first-party Drive + co-editing.
- Mature, polished, with native clients.

## Where Canopy differs

- Canopy isn't a NAS and won't manage storage hardware — in fact it runs **well as an app on top of
  one** (point it at the NAS filesystem or its S3-compatible endpoint).
- Edge-deployable, vendor-neutral, open source; bring-your-own identity.

## Pick Synology if

You want a turnkey storage appliance with backups, RAID, and a broad app suite. Use Canopy as the
drive / portal layer over it if you like.
