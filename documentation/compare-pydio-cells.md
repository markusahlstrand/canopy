# Pydio Cells

> A Go-based self-hosted document-sharing platform aimed at teams and enterprises.

## What it is

A self-hosted file-sharing and management platform (a Go rewrite of the older PHP Pydio),
organized around "cells" — shared workspaces — with granular permissions, workflows, sync, and an
admin console. It targets business and enterprise use.

## Where it beats Canopy

- Mature permissions, workspaces, and governance / admin features.
- Desktop sync, mobile apps, workflow automation.
- An enterprise track record and commercial support.

## Where Canopy differs

- Lighter footprint and edge-serverless deployment; bring-your-own object storage.
- A plugin platform on a slim core; open and hackable.
- Canopy is early; Pydio is established and enterprise-leaning.

## Pick Pydio Cells if

You want a structured, enterprise-oriented self-hosted document platform with strong access
control.
